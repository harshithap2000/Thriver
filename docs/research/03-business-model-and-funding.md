# 03 – Business model & funding

## 1. Business Model Canvas (first draft)

| Block                      | Draft                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Customer segments**      | _Users:_ adult cancer survivors in India after curative-intent treatment, starting with breast cancer, then head and neck and colorectal; their family caregivers. _Payers:_ private cancer hospitals and chains; health insurers; oncology pharma; CSR foundations and NGOs; corporates (employee benefit).                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Value propositions**     | _Patients:_ a safe, clinician-approved recovery plan for exercise, food and daily life; all records in one place, explained in plain words; family support; less fear and isolation. _Hospitals:_ better continuity of care after discharge, fewer avoidable readmissions, survivorship programmes without new staff, patient retention and loyalty. _Insurers:_ healthier members, lower claims, a wellness benefit that differentiates. _Pharma:_ adherence and outcomes data (consented), patient-support programme infrastructure. |
| **Channels**               | Hospital referral at discharge (QR code on the discharge summary); oncologists and physiotherapists; NGOs and support groups; insurer wellness apps; content (YouTube, Instagram in Indian languages); survivor ambassadors.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Customer relationships** | Daily companion app; human onco-physio and dietitian check-ins (paid tier or institution-funded); moderated community; family notes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Revenue streams**        | See section 2.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Key resources**          | Clinically approved protocol library; clinical advisory board; AI and records platform; trust (privacy, safety record); Indian-language content.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Key activities**         | Protocol authoring and review; hospital partnerships; regulatory and privacy compliance; outcome studies; community moderation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Key partners**           | Cancer hospitals (Tata Memorial network, HCG, Apollo, regional centres); HCAH-type rehab providers; NGOs (Indian Cancer Society, CanSupport, V Care); onco-physiotherapists and dietitians; insurers; pharma; ABDM ecosystem.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Cost structure**         | Clinical staff and advisers; engineering; AI API usage (roughly per question; see the records assistant); compliance (legal, security audits); content production; partnership sales.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## 2. Who pays

Indian cancer families already face severe out-of-pocket costs (59% catastrophic spending including
indirect costs; see [02](02-market-and-competitors.md#financial-toxicity-critical-for-the-business-model)).
So the core app should be **free or very cheap for patients**, funded mainly by institutions.

**Avoid:** selling supplements or nutraceuticals. It conflicts with "clinically validated" positioning
and is where some competitors (for example ZenOnco) earn revenue, so avoiding it is also a
differentiator.

## 5. The Tata question

- **Ratan Tata passed away in October 2024**, so a personal pitch to him is no longer possible.
- The **UC-RNT Fund** (RNT Associates with the University of California; once planned at $300M) is
  reported to be **winding down**. The report's date is unclear.
  ([Venture Intelligence](https://news.ventureintelligence.com/private-equity/ratan-tata-backed-uc-rnt-fund-to-wind-down))
- Better routes into the Tata ecosystem:
  - **Tata Memorial Centre** (India's leading cancer centre network): a clinical or research
    partnership for a pilot study.
  - **Tata Trusts**, which run major cancer-care initiatives: a grant or partnership for patient access
    (to research next).
  - **Tata Medical Center, Kolkata**: a clinical partner.

## 6. Suggested sequence

1. **Now:** recruit a clinical advisory board (onco-physio, onco-dietitian, oncologist). Run a
   trademark check. Get DPIIT recognition.
2. **Pilot (6 months):** one partner hospital, breast cancer survivors, free for patients. Measure
   adherence, quality of life (for example FACT-G), fatigue and patient satisfaction.
3. **Raise:** grant (BIRAC or a research partnership) plus pre-seed from angels or a health-focused
   seed fund, backed by pilot data.
4. **Revenue:** hospital SaaS contracts, then insurer and pharma partnerships, plus CSR-funded access
   for low-income patients.
